Software & Data

I build and lead open-data communities and FAIR data tooling for the water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) sector, and I write and manage the grants that fund them.

Grants & Funding

Wrote and managed a portfolio of ETH Board Open Research Data grants worth roughly CHF 500,000 in total (2023-2026).

openwashdata - establishing Data Stewards and increasing FAIRness

ETH Board ORD Program (Explore, 2nd call)

2024 - Current

  • Wrote the proposal and manage the project (Prof. Elizabeth Tilley, PI). Places funded data stewards in partner organisations (UKZN, South Africa; BASEflow, Malawi) and delivers a 12-module data stewardship training programme.

washcollab - Enhancing Global WASH Data Accessibility through Collaborative Initiatives

ETH Board ORD Program (Contribute)

2024 - Current

  • Wrote the proposal and manage the project (Prof. Elizabeth Tilley, PI). A partnership between WASHWeb and openwashdata maintaining open-source tools for authoritative WASH data (WHO/UNICEF JMP, World Bank, UN-Water GLAAS, OECD).

FAIRqual - FAIR Data Practices for Qualitative Research in Transdisciplinarity

ETH Board ORD Program (Explore, Measure 1)

2024 - 2025

  • Co-wrote the proposal and contributed to the project (team across TdLab and Global Health Engineering, ETH Zurich). Develops tools and standards for sharing hard-to-anonymise qualitative research data following FAIR principles.

openwashdata - Open WASH data by building Open Science Competencies and Community

ETH Board ORD Program (Explore, 1st call)

2023 - 2024

  • Wrote the proposal and managed the project (Prof. Elizabeth Tilley, PI). Built the infrastructure, community and FAIR data publishing workflow for open WASH data; 18 months.

openjmp - Open JMP: unlocking the potential of global indicator data

ETH Board ORD Program (Contribute)

2023 - 2024

  • Wrote the proposal and managed the project (Prof. Elizabeth Tilley, PI). In partnership with the WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme, turns decades of WASH coverage data into documented open R packages; 15 months.
